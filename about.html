"Astrology does not remove responsibility. It sharpens it."

I didn't come to Vedic astrology as an intellectual pursuit. I came because the same questions kept repeating in my own life, and conventional answers stopped working.

Loss, prolonged uncertainty, and periods of internal conflict shaped important phases of my life. Decisions felt heavier than situations warranted. Relationships carried familiar tensions despite changing circumstances. I kept wondering: why does this keep happening?

Astrology entered my life not as belief or escape, but as a way to understand why similar situations kept resurfacing, why timing mattered more than effort in certain phases, and why some problems resolved only when approached differently. What I found wasn't vague symbolism. It was a rigorous framework that explained patterns I had already lived through, including their timing.

Vedic astrology is a predictive system thousands of years old, used to understand repeating life patterns, decision timing, and periods of challenge or opportunity. Charts reveal why one person repeatedly attracts unstable partnerships, why another faces career stagnation despite competence, or why health issues surface under stress rather than randomly. A birth chart shows what's already set in motion, and when particular themes become active.

Vedic astrology works at a more fundamental level than therapy or coaching. Therapy processes emotions. Coaching drives action. Astrology addresses the structure beneath both, the deeper patterns and timing cycles that explain why certain themes persist, why effort works in some phases and not others, and why specific challenges recur across a lifetime. This is why many people find clarity through astrology even after years of inner work or goal-setting. It doesn't begin with symptoms or outcomes. It begins with the underlying design and its movement through time.

Prediction here exists for preparation, not certainty. Some events unfold regardless of effort. Others change significantly based on timing, response, and restraint. You won't hear dramatic pronouncements or guaranteed timelines. You'll hear what you're likely dealing with, and what matters in how you engage it.

My work focuses on identifying patterns that repeat unless addressed, periods when life pushes for change, and phases where restraint prevents damage.

I use natal, divisional, transit charts, and planetary periods to understand what's likely to unfold, how intensely it may express, and what response reduces unnecessary loss. Charts point to specific periods, triggers, and outcomes, not just themes. This allows people to prepare rather than react.

Some patterns soften through effort. Others must be navigated with preparation rather than resistance.

Important: Astrology doesn't replace doctors, therapists, or professional advice. It flags vulnerabilities before they harden, so corrective steps can be taken in time.

I offer clarity that helps people make better decisions when the stakes are real, along with compassion for what they're carrying.

A consultation addresses questions people often struggle to resolve on their own: whether to change jobs now or wait, why the same relationship dynamics repeat despite new partners, why family pressure intensifies at certain ages, or why health issues flare during specific periods.

Sessions are collaborative. You're encouraged to ask questions and engage actively rather than receive answers passively.

You leave with: specific insight into your situation, clearer priorities for the phase you're in, and concrete guidance you can act on, with renewed agency over your own life.

Most people come when they feel stuck or overwhelmed. But clarity often matters most before decisions become urgent.

My approach is shaped by how I've lived and worked. Years in product management trained me to evaluate trade-offs, long-term consequences, and imperfect decisions made under pressure. Training in social sciences deepened my understanding of power dynamics, emotional conditioning, and behavioural patterns. Over a decade of Vipassana practice strengthened my ability to observe without reacting, to sit with complexity without forcing answers. This lens allows me to read charts without projection or exaggeration, examining patterns in relation to real life circumstances, not isolated symbols.

I hold your questions with care. The work we do together is confidential, serious, and without judgment.
